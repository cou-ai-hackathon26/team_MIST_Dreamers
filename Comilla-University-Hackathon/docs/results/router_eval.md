## Notes

- Best local router on this test set: **BanglaBERT**; the app uses it with `ROUTER_BACKEND`.
- Questions below the threshold go to the LLM with the router's top 3 labels as hints (FR-AGT-03), so the agent's final routing is the "after LLM" column.
- A confident wrong route is not the end of the story: the agent then grades the passages and verifies every claim (FR-AGT-06, 10), so a question the documents of that service do not cover ends in the fixed fallback text instead of a wrong answer.
- Inference is measured on this laptop's CPU, one question at a time, after the model was loaded (target at most 200 ms, NFR-PERF-03).
- The SRS target is at least 90% accuracy (SRS 11).
